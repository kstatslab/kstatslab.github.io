ScholarGauge – privacy policy

Effective 28 October 2026, updated 13 November 2026 (version 1.1) · Developer: KStats Lab (kstatslab@gmail.com)

ScholarGauge has no server and collects nothing about you. It is a browser extension that adds journal quartiles, citation facts and study-design tags to Google Scholar™ pages.

Your consent

After installation, ScholarGauge shows what it reads and sends, and looks nothing up until you choose Agree and turn on. You can switch look-ups off at any time in its Options.

What the extension reads

Google Scholar pages (search results and profile pages): paper titles, authors, journal names, years and citation counts as shown on the page, and on a profile the name, affiliation and Scholar's citation totals and citations-per-year chart.

On other websites it reads nothing by itself. Only when you select text or right-click a DOI link and choose Check with ScholarGauge does it receive that text or link from the browser, to look the paper up.

In the Firefox edition, Firefox itself asks at installation for permission to send website content and search terms (the first four rows), and asks again before an e-mail address or key you type in is used; you can change these choices in Firefox's add-on settings.

All requests use HTTPS. Nothing is sent to the developer or to anyone else. There is no analytics, advertising, tracking, profiling, or sale or transfer of data.

What it keeps

Your settings (consent, OpenAlex key, Crossref e-mail, Web of Science key, switches), any SCImago files you import, cached look-ups (title → DOI for 4 months, OpenAlex records for 2 weeks, Web of Science answers for 30 days, searches for 1 day) and a count of today's requests are stored only in your browser's extension storage. Remove them with Options → Clear cache, or by removing the extension.

Profiles you choose to save (with Save profile or Compare): the name, affiliation and Scholar address of the profile, the numbers shown on its cards and its list of papers (title, year, citations, journal and quartile). The last profile you open as a one-page report is kept the same way. They stay in your browser, are never sent anywhere, and are removed with Delete on the Saved profiles page or by removing the extension. The Journal tab of the Research lab uses only the journal data built into the extension; nothing you type there is sent.

Third-party services

Crossref and OpenAlex are public scholarly databases with their own terms and privacy policies. Clarivate's Web of Science API is used only with a key you obtain from Clarivate yourself, under Clarivate's terms; ScholarGauge asks your browser for access to api.clarivate.com only when you save such a key, and you can withdraw it with Options → Remove key. The journal data (SCImago Journal & Country Rank) is bundled in the extension; no request is made to SCImago. ScholarGauge is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google, SCImago, Elsevier, Clarivate, Crossref or OpenAlex. Google Scholar is a trademark of Google LLC.

Use of data

The use of information received from these services follows the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements, and Mozilla's Add-on Policies: data is used only to show the facts described above, inside your browser.

Changes

Any change to these practices will be described here with a new date, and shown in the extension, before it takes effect.
